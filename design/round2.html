Round 2: your favourite pieces, in six looks

Every look below has the same seven screens, built from the parts you picked in round 1. The only differences are type, colour and shape, so you can compare like for like. Tap the body, the severity buttons, Front/Back and the symptom tiles to try them. All data is made up.

Today: Ebb's cycle ringCycle day in the middle and one big Log button. Below it, today's pain logs in time order, so you can see where the pain moved.
Pain: front and back body mapTap every place it hurts, choose Mild, Moderate or Severe, and pick the type. Log again later in the day and the timeline shows how it moved.
Twinge's “Add what else happened”Tap a tile to add it, and tap it again to remove it. The open card has Edit instead of Remove: add your own options, rename them or delete them. There's also a tile for making a new symptom class.
Twinge's calendarSeverity bars, a dot for each symptom class you logged, and a tint on bleeding days.
Summary (just for you)Ebb's summary card, now for you: headline numbers, where it hurts most (body heat map) and what tends to come with it.
For your doctorTwinge's tick-what-to-include export. The preview shows the body map, and how the pain moved on a bad day.
Almanac and Flare are gone, as you asked. Every look uses the same logic, so whichever you choose, the app works the same. You can also swap a colour or font from one look into another.
